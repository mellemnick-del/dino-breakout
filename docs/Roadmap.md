# Dino Breakout roadmap

Ideas we've agreed are worth building, in the order we plan to ship them. Real player data (where people quit, which parks they replay, which ads they watch) can reorder this.

## 1.0: in review
Everything in the README: 7 parks, 6 towers with branching upgrades, 10 dinos, endless mode, daily challenge, Research Lab, save and resume, iPhone and iPad.

## 1.1: optional ads (built, on the `version-1.1` branch)
- Rewarded ads only: revive, bonus cash between waves (every 5 waves), double amber at game over
- Fixes and balance from TestFlight feedback
- Ships after 1.0 is approved. See `docs/ReleaseChecklist.md`, "Version 1.1: rewarded ads"

## 1.2: growth (cheap wins)
- **Translations:** Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Japanese. Game text plus the App Store listing in each language.
- **Shareable result cards:** a Share button at game over that makes an image of your actual maze, the park, the wave you reached, and the difficulty.
- **Weekend events:** limited-time rule sets built on the daily challenge system, like "Raptor Rampage weekend: raptors only, double amber."

## 1.3: the big content update
- **New bosses:**
  - **Mosasaurus:** surfaces in rivers and lunges at nearby towers.
  - **Quetzalcoatlus:** a flyer boss; only a strong flak line brings it down.
  - **Giganotosaurus:** a ground boss that smashes towers in its path.
- **Hero unit: the Radio Operator.** A park ranger you drag around the board. Charge the radio and call in support: artillery on a spot, a supply drop of cash, or a chopper gun run. Adds a skill layer most mazing games don't have.

## Later
- **Park Builder:** design a park (gate, helipad, terrain, rules, dino mix), share it with a short code, and challenge friends to beat your wave. Includes a "clear check": you must survive wave 10 on your own park before sharing. Best once there's an audience sharing codes.
- **Home screen widget:** today's daily challenge and your streak.
- **Featured parks:** a "Park of the Week" from the community.
