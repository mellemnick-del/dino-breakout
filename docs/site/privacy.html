Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

We don't collect your personal data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no analytics. Your progress, saved games, settings, best waves, research, and achievements are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game submits your endless mode and daily challenge scores to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. That is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive your Game Center information.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
