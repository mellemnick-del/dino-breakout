Common questions

How do I unlock more parks? Reach wave 10 on a park, on any difficulty, to unlock the next one.

How do I play endless mode? Beat all 50 waves on a park. Its card on the main menu then shows an Endless button, or tap Keep Going on the victory screen.

How do I upgrade a tower? Tap the tower to open its panel, then tap Upgrade. The same panel has the tower's special ability and its Aim setting.

Can I see the tutorial again? Yes: Settings, then Replay the tutorial. It plays the next time you start Paddock B.

The sound is off. Check the Music and Sound effects sliders in Settings, and your iPhone's silent switch. The game follows it.

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your device, and runs save between waves. Deleting the app deletes it.
