Tower defense for iPhone and iPad

Dino Breakout

The paddocks are open. Hold the line until the choppers arrive.

Dino Breakout is a maze-building tower defense game. Every fence and tower you place is a wall, and the dinosaurs always take the shortest way left to the rescue chopper. Build a long, twisting maze, line it with tranq rifles, turrets, flares, flak cannons, and shock pylons, and survive 50 waves across seven parks. Ten kinds of dinosaur, from swarming compies to fence-smashing ankylosaurs and the T-Rex.

No account needed and it plays offline. No ads.
